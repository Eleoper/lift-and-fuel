# The plan

This is the owner's personal plan as of October 2026, and it's the content the app ships with. Height and age (5'5", 23) come from an earlier program and should be confirmed.

## Where things stand

| | Now | Long-term goal |
|---|---|---|
| Weight | 180 lb | 190 lb |
| Body fat | 29% (≈52 lb fat, ≈128 lb lean) | 15% |

Reaching 190 lb at 15% would take about 33 lb of new lean mass, which is a multi-year project for a natural lifter. The plan is to **cut first**: reaching 15% likely lands around 150–155 lb in 6–8 months, followed by a slow lean bulk.

## Training: the hybrid sweet spot (3 runs + 3 lifts)

| Day | Session | Details |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | Upper Push, 40–50 min | Bench or DB press 4×6–8 · Incline DB press 3×8–10 · Shoulder press 3×6–8 · Dips or triceps pushdowns 3×10–12 · Lateral raises 3×12–15 |
| Tue | Speed Run, 45–60 min | Pick one: 6×400 m at 5K pace (90 s rest) · 10×200 m fast (walk/jog recovery) · 20 min tempo, comfortably hard |
| Wed | Upper Pull, 40–50 min | Pull-ups or lat pulldown 4×6–10 · Barbell or seated row 3×6–8 · Face pulls 3×12–15 · Hammer curls 3×10–12 · Reverse fly 3×12–15 |
| Thu | Easy Run, 30–50 min | Zone 2, conversational, about +1–2 min/km on interval pace |
| Fri | Lower Body, 40–50 min | Squat or leg press 3×5–6 · Romanian deadlift 3×6–8 · Bulgarian split squat 2×8 each · Leg curls 2×12–15 · Calf raises 2×15–20 |
| Sat | Long Run, 60–120 min | Easy pace, practice fueling |
| Sun | Off / Mobility | Recovery, mobility, easy walk, weekly check-in |

The rules: lift heavy, stay controlled, and leave 1–2 reps in reserve. When you hit the top of the rep range on every set, add weight next time (+5 lb upper body, +10 lb lower body). Keep Friday legs easy enough that Saturday's long run doesn't suffer. Build the long run gradually, starting at 45–60 min and adding about 10 min a week.

## What to track

- Weigh in daily and judge the **7-day average** (aim for −1 to −1.5 lb a week). Do it right after waking, after the bathroom, before food or water, wearing the same clothes, on the same scale.
- Measure **waist at the navel and neck** weekly, relaxed and in the morning. The app turns these into a U.S. Navy tape body-fat estimate (±3–4%) and a waist-to-height ratio (aim for under 0.5).
- Get a **DEXA scan** every 8–12 weeks if possible. It's the most accurate practical body-fat measure. Enter the result in the scan box, and it overrides the tape estimate.
- Treat **BMI** as the least useful number. It can't tell muscle from fat.
- Take **progress photos** every 2 weeks.
- Check that **strength on the main lifts** holds or climbs.
- Log **body fat %** monthly, using the same method each time.
- Track **steps, sleep and hunger/energy**.
